# 🧾 BigMart Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Project Overview
The *BigMart Sales Analysis Dashboard* provides insights into the sales performance of various product categories across different outlets. Built in *Microsoft Power BI*, this interactive dashboard helps analyze key sales metrics and identify patterns to support data-driven retail decisions.

---

## 🧠 Objectives
- Analyze total outlet sales by *item type* and *establishment year*  
- Identify top-performing product categories and outlets  
- Track overall sales trends and performance metrics  
- Provide a quick overview of *total sales volume* across the company  

---

## ⚙ Tools and Technologies
- *Power BI* – for dashboard creation and visualization  
- *Microsoft Excel / CSV* – as the data source  
- *DAX* – for calculated measures and KPIs  

---

## 🧩 Key Insights Visualized
1. *Sum of Item Outlet Sales by Item Type*  
   - Highlights which product categories (e.g., Fruits, Snacks, Dairy) generate the highest sales.  
2. *Total Sales KPI*  
   - Displays the overall company sales performance (e.g., 14.96M total sales).  
3. *Item Outlet Sales by Establishment Year*  
   - Analyzes how outlet establishment year affects sales trends.  
4. *Count of Outlet Type by Item Outlet Sales*  
   - Visualizes the distribution of outlet types and their contribution to total sales.  
5. *Pie Chart of Item Outlet Sales*  
   - Compares product category sales contribution as a percentage of total sales.  

---

## 🗂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains the following key columns:
- Item_Identifier – Unique ID for each product  
- Item_Type – Product category (e.g., Snacks, Dairy, Frozen Foods)  
- Outlet_Identifier – Unique store ID  
- Outlet_Establishment_Year – Year the outlet was opened  
- Item_MRP – Maximum retail price of the item  
- Item_Outlet_Sales – Total sales for the product at the outlet  
---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Open the .pbix file in *Power BI Desktop*.  
2. Connect to the *BigMart sales dataset (CSV or Excel)*.  
3. Refresh the data and explore the dashboard filters and visuals.
